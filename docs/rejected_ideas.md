# Deferred / Rejected Productivity Ideas

This document logs productivity feature ideas that were evaluated and marked as **not wanted** or **out of scope** for VedWriter to avoid cluttering core development.

---

## Rejected Ideas

### 1. Command Palette (`Ctrl+K` / `Cmd+K`)
* **Concept:** Single keyboard shortcut to trigger fuzzy-search modal for navigating pages, switching journals, and running app actions.
* **Ease of Dev:** Easy
* **Complexity:** Low
* **Usefulness Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)
* **Status:** Rejected / Not Wanted.

### 2. Zen / Auto-Dim Typewriter Mode
* **Concept:** Auto-dims surrounding text and hides sidebars when typing begins; centers current line vertically.
* **Ease of Dev:** Very Easy
* **Complexity:** Low
* **Usefulness Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)
* **Status:** Rejected / Not Wanted.

### 3. Inline Wiki-linking (`[[Page Title]]`)
* **Concept:** Typing `[[` opens page lookup and links or auto-creates pages inline.
* **Ease of Dev:** Medium
* **Complexity:** Medium
* **Usefulness Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)
* **Status:** Rejected / Not Wanted.

### 4. Natural Language Date Shortcuts (`@today`, `@tomorrow`)
* **Concept:** Parses inline strings like `@today` or `@next monday` into formatted date tags/links.
* **Ease of Dev:** Medium
* **Complexity:** Low
* **Usefulness Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)
* **Status:** Rejected / Not Wanted.

### 5. Auto-Organizing Scratchpad
* **Concept:** Temporary, unassigned buffer for raw notes without requiring a title or folder first.
* **Ease of Dev:** Medium
* **Complexity:** Medium
* **Usefulness Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5)
* **Status:** Rejected / Not Wanted.
